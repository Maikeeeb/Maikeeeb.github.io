Why build it?

For a closer technical review

Decisions & tradeoffs.

Open each note for the implementation boundary, tradeoff, and next engineering question.

01Heuristics change the search strategy

The score combines accumulated cost with Euclidean distance, then casts to an integer. The weights change exploration, and quantization creates ties. Both matter when interpreting timing comparisons.

02A found route does not prove optimality

The implementation marks positions when enqueuing them and does not reopen better paths. It is a weighted-search experiment, not a guarantee of shortest paths. A small-grid BFS oracle would provide a stronger correctness comparison.
