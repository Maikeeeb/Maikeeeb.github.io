Decisions & tradeoffs.

Open each note for the implementation boundary, tradeoff, and next engineering question.

01Events reduce direct coupling

C# delegates/events let road generation notify obstacle spawning without embedding every spawn rule in the generator. Subscription lifetime still matters: reused or destroyed objects must not leave stale listeners behind.

02Coroutines express timed behavior

Movement and blinking unfold over frames. That is readable, but overlapping coroutines can create conflicting transitions. A developed controller should define cancellation and state ownership explicitly.
