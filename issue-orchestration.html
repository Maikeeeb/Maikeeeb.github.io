For a closer technical review

Decisions & tradeoffs.

Open each note for the implementation boundary, tradeoff, and next engineering question.

01Worktrees do not isolate every side effect

Separate checkouts prevent routine file collisions, but agents may still share ports, databases, caches, and remote resources. Coordination must treat those resources explicitly and serialize integration where necessary.

02Bound retries and preserve evidence

A fixed review/fix loop makes repeated failure visible. Structured handoffs carry changed files, results, and unresolved risks. Passing a gate is useful evidence; it does not remove the need to understand the change.
